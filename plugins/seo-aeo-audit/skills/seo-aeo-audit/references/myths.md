# Myth guard — claims that do not survive 2026 evidence

Say this plainly when asked, offer the nearest thing that works, move on.

| Claim | Reality | What to do instead |
|---|---|---|
| "`llms.txt` improves AI visibility" | Google states it has no effect on Search (neither positive nor negative). In a 100M-site experiment it attracted 0.1% of AI-bot traffic and performed 3× worse than normal pages; in a 54-study meta-analysis it scores 2.0/10 (no evidence). Chrome's Lighthouse *does* check for it under agentic-browsing audits — a different surface, and optional | Keep the HTML clean and fetchable. Publish `llms.txt` only if you are targeting agentic browsers, and never bill it as a ranking or citation lever |
| "Serve Markdown mirrors of your pages for LLMs" | Markdown mirrors received **0%** AI-crawler visits versus 4.6% for the HTML versions; ChatGPT Deep Research does not follow `.md` links. One single-site test reported AIO gains — the evidence conflicts | Improve the HTML: front-loaded answer, plain-text facts, fewer links before the content. If you still want to test Markdown, do it as a measured experiment on one section |
| "Chunk your content for the retriever" | Chunk length, overlap and boundaries are chosen by the engine, not the publisher; Google says chunking is unnecessary. What does travel is one clear claim per passage | Write self-contained passages that each make one point |
| "Rewrite the text specifically for AI" | The one controlled academic benchmark found gains from citing authoritative sources, quoting experts, adding statistics and improving readability — keyword-style manipulation scored below baseline | Add evidence and clarity; that serves both audiences |
| "More schema = more AI citations" (canonical stance: schema is an **eligibility and entity aid on specific surfaces**, never a ranking or citation lever — mark up what is real and required, then stop; see aeo-geo.md F6, entity-and-brand.md G3, onpage-checks.md D1) | Google calls excessive Schema.org useless; a 10-week controlled test of LocalBusiness schema across 29 domains found no ranking or visibility gain in Google/Bing/Yahoo (Maps got worse); a large GEO experiment saw schema lift Google AIO but **reduce** citations in ChatGPT, Gemini and Copilot | Mark up what is real and required for the features you actually want (products, jobs, events, breadcrumbs); stop there |
| "Add FAQPage markup for rich results" | Google discontinued FAQPage/Question/Answer rich results entirely | Answer the questions in the page body; use FAQ structure for users and extractability, not for a SERP feature |
| "AMP gives a ranking advantage" | The AMP cache is off, Search redirects to the publisher's AMP host, and AMP ranks like any other page | Optimize the normal page |
| "Update the publish date to refresh the page" | Google fingerprints pages and diffs them: title changes, substantial new sections and link changes count; date/`lastmod` bumps, JS, ads and boilerplate do not. Freshness is also relative to the SERP's age distribution, not the calendar | Do a substantial update, triggered by the age-versus-SERP check |
| "Publish more pages to grow" | The index is capacity-bound and quality-gated; adding pages dilutes and competes for finite slots. Fewer, better-indexed pages routinely win | Index tiering, consolidation, information gain |
| "Disavow anything a toxicity tool flags" | Penguin 4.0 devalues rather than demotes; third-party toxicity scores collapse context into one number and cause self-inflicted losses | Disavow only for a manual action, a dated negative-SEO spike with ranking loss, a known bought-link blast, or inherited spam |
| "Track one AI-visibility score" | Only ~2.4% of URLs are cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AIO simultaneously; the same brand's visibility differs by engine, persona and prompt format | Measure per engine and persona: mention, recommendation, then citation |
| "Publish a 'best [category]' listicle with yourself at #1" | When the model used a brand's own self-promotional article as its source, the brand was excluded from the recommendation in **69%** of cases; Google appears to have decoupled who is cited from who is recommended, and organic visibility of sites leaning on the tactic fell from early 2026 | Earn third-party placements; publish comparisons where you would be a natural pick if a stranger had written them |
| "Scale AI content for coverage" | Documented penalty profile: a directory of 850k AI articles took a "Scaled content abuse" action and lost both Google visibility and ChatGPT citations; 800+ generated pages preceded a 50k→10k monthly-visit collapse elsewhere; pure AI content ranks briefly then falls | Human-edited AI beats pure generation ~5×; ship fewer pages with original data |
| "Getting cited is the goal" | Users click a link inside an AI summary in roughly 1% of sessions, and a citation can point elsewhere while the answer names a competitor | Optimize for being **named and recommended**, and measure business outcomes (calls, signups, bookings) |
| "Rank #1 in Google and AI visibility follows" | Rank is the strongest single correlate, but classic signals explain only 15–20% of ChatGPT recommendation variance, and a Bing-only site earned 90k+ ChatGPT citations | Treat AI visibility as a portfolio: rank somewhere, be extractable, be corroborated |
| "Blocked-but-indexed URLs are diluting my site" | Google indexes the URL string without processing content — those pages cannot trigger duplicate filters or dilute quality | Leave the block in place; only act if they steal impressions from a canonical page |
| "A crawler hit means the model learned us" | Crawled → archived → extracted → filtered → deduplicated → mixed → trained. Content drops out at every stage, and deployed weights are frozen until retraining | Measure retrieval and citation, not crawler logs, for AI knowledge |
| "Optimize for `unicornClicks`" | It marks child accounts (COPPA/GDPR-K isolation), not premium users | Ignore it |
| "There are 200 ranking factors" | No such list exists; the number traces to one 2009 conference remark. Modern ranking runs hundreds-to-thousands of features plus ML overlays, and weighting is query-dependent (for scale, the Yandex leak showed ~690 factors) | Diagnose per query class on this site: what does this SERP reward, and what is this page missing (ranking-model.md) |
| "E-E-A-T is a ranking factor — raise our score" | E-E-A-T comes from the rater guidelines; raters evaluate, they do not rank. There is no E-E-A-T dial | Treat it as the specification of quality: demonstrated first-hand experience, named authors with evidence, transparent method — strongest requirement in YMYL |
| "AI wrote it, we just need to publish it" | Unedited AI content fails trust checks, readers increasingly detect it, and pure generation was beaten ~5× by AI-assisted human editing in controlled tests; scaled generation is a documented penalty profile | Keep AI for research, structure and analysis; keep the judgement, evidence and voice human, and verify every statistic |
| "Last-click attribution tells us what organic did" | The journey now spans AI answers, social, desktop research and phone calls; whole channels (calls, offline, AI referrals) sit outside the model, and one business found 80–90% of a channel's conversions arrived by phone | Multi-touch plus call tracking and offline conversion import; report branded search and direct as outcome metrics (demand-and-conversion.md) |
| "Google wants to see it there" | If the only justification for an element is what an engine supposedly wants, it is the wrong decision — pages that hold up are built from user expectation, and made-for-search-engines content is exactly what the helpful-content systems demote | Write down what a user expects on this page type, ship that, then check crawlability |
| "This quarter's framework is the thing to chase" | The industry produces a new buzzword every quarter; the brands winning in 2026 built authority before the panic and executed one strategy for years | Separate a fundamental shift from temporary noise — not every new tactic deserves a strategy overhaul |
| "SEO can fix AEO on its own" | The work spans brand, PR, product and review platforms; models pick names from memory and then look for support | Split the ownership explicitly in the plan |

## How to say it

Short, factual, no lecture, then the alternative:

> `llms.txt` will not change Google rankings or AI citations — Google documents
> that it is ignored, and in the largest published test the files drew 0.1% of
> AI-bot traffic. What did move citations in the same test was clean HTML,
> earned distribution and human-edited content. I have put those in the plan; if
> you want `llms.txt` for agentic browsers we can ship it as a five-minute
> extra, measured separately.
