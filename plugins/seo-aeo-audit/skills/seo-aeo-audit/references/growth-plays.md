# The play list — what to put in the plan, ranked

Each play: the trigger that justifies it, the change, the mechanism, the observed
effect, the evidence tier, and how to verify. Only recommend a play when its
trigger is present in **this** audit. Nothing here relies on deception; the
adversarial material lives in threats-and-defense.md as defense only.

Tiers: `CONFIRMED` (documented by the engine or reproduced here) · `STUDY`
(published multi-site data) · `FIELD` (single-case report) · `HYPOTHESIS`.

## Blockers — do these before anything else

| # | Trigger | Play | Mechanism | Observed | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | Manual action present | Fix every cited cause, document, then one reconsideration request | The action is a binary multiplier on ranking | Recovery within 24h–weeks once accepted | CONFIRMED |
| B2 | `noindex` in pre-render source, or `meta refresh` + `noindex` | Replace with a server-side 301; strip the directive | `noindex` is a directive and wins over canonical; it counts if present in either source or DOM | Page returns to index within a crawl cycle | CONFIRMED |
| B3 | Rendering resources blocked (`/_next`, `/assets`, CSS/JS) | Allow the render-critical paths | Blocked resources are never downloaded, so never rendered | Reindexing and partial traffic recovery within weeks | FIELD |
| B4 | Canonical carries `media`/`type`/`hreflang`/`lang` | Emit `rel` + `href` only | Extra attributes make Google discard the declaration | URL Inspection shows the user-declared canonical accepted | CONFIRMED |
| B5 | Broad `robots.txt` wildcard (`/*?`, `/*print`) | Replace with parameter-specific rules; canonicalize filtered variants | Wildcards match substrings, not paths | One store recovered ~40% of product pages; a retailer's 45% traffic loss traced to one line | FIELD |
| B6 | Soft 404s at scale / auto-generated empty pages | Return real 404/410, remove or noindex the generators, tighten parameters | Soft 404s degrade host quality and make Google abandon crawling | −83% soft 404s; crawl requests and Discover share recovered first | FIELD |
| B7 | Hijacked or dangling subdomain | Reclaim DNS, verify every property variant in GSC, monitor both www/non-www | Abandoned CNAMEs get claimed and redirected | Full recovery in 36h in the documented case | FIELD |
| B8 | Bulk `noindex` shipped on advice; GSC "Excluded by 'noindex' tag" holds URLs that used to have impressions | Restore in batches, strongest content first, ~2 weeks apart; resubmit each batch, keep only indexable URLs in the sitemap; fix the thin templates editorially (short lead explaining the data, one takeaway line, then the table) | Googlebot stops visiting noindexed URLs, so removing the tag triggers nothing by itself — recovery needs re-discovery plus a host-quality reassessment | A sports site fell from 4–5k daily impressions to ~10; 6–12 weeks to recover once crawling restarted | FIELD |
| B9 | Retrieval crawlers blocked in `robots.txt`, WAF or CDN bot rules while AI visibility is a stated goal | Decide per agent and enforce it deliberately: allow the retrieval bots you want (OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot), keep Googlebot unblocked, verify in logs with forward-confirmed reverse DNS | Retrieval bots are separate from training bots — unblocking `GPTBot` does nothing for `OAI-SearchBot`; a blocked page returns `viewing lines [0-0] of 0` to ChatGPT Deep Research and drops out of the answer silently | Sites that lost 85% of Google traffic lost 75% of ChatGPT traffic and blocking Googlebot cuts ChatGPT proportionally (FIELD) — a Bing-only counter-case points the other way, so the *index dependency* is HYPOTHESIS (aeo-geo.md F4). The play itself does not depend on resolving it: agent separation is documented | CONFIRMED (agent separation); the dependency figure is FIELD |
| B10 | `nosnippet`, `data-nosnippet` or `max-snippet:0` on pages the business wants quoted | Remove preview suppression from money and answer pages; keep it only where a paywall or legal reason is real | Preview control gates whether any engine may show or quote the passage at all | Preview control scores 9.2/10 in the 54-study meta-analysis of AI-citation factors — the highest-weighted lever the publisher fully controls | STUDY |
| B11 | Migration, replatform or domain move inside the decline window — or one being planned | Run the eight-stage protocol in technical-checks.md; file a change-of-address for **every** old variant, including forgotten subdomains and www/non-www; watch the old host in logs until Googlebot stops fetching it | Address and redirect signals consolidate only when every variant is declared; an unreported variant keeps its own crawl and index state | Average, disciplined and worst-case migration losses: benchmarks.md, "Operational benchmarks". Undeclared subdomains keep burning crawl budget even when noindexed | CONFIRMED |

## Leaks — stop the bleeding

| # | Trigger | Play | Mechanism | Observed | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Crawl budget on filters/params (log evidence) | Facet/filter split, parameter rules, canonical to base, cap pagination | Budget follows structure and quality signals | Product crawl rate ×4; new products indexed in 2 days vs 3 weeks | FIELD |
| L2 | Large index with low-value pages | Index tiering (must/should/block/hard-block) by business, search and user value — the tier definitions, the scoring rule and the running metrics are owned by **technical-checks.md A2** | Fewer, better pages concentrate budget and quality | The 50k-page store case (indexed counts before/after, zero-traffic pages −87%, organic +67% in 90 days): technical-checks.md A2 | FIELD |
| L3 | Homepage hoards equity, money pages starve | Link products/services from the homepage; comparison and solution hubs; contextual links from traffic pages | Equity flows by count, position, relevance and source authority | Product pages +12 positions average, +$340K organic revenue in 90 days | FIELD |
| L4 | Same query on multiple URLs, >70% SERP overlap | Merge: content to the winner, 301 the losers, update internal links | Rotational testing splits clicks between candidates | Combined clicks rise once one source is established | STUDY |
| L5 | 60+ links before the content in source order | Trim sitewide navigation to top categories; subcategories on their own pages; add an HTML sitemap | Every link marker spends the first-read budget (window and link-count bands: architecture-and-equity.md) | ~90% link cut improved rankings in Google **and** ChatGPT | FIELD |
| L6 | Equity into tag archives, calendars, author bios, internal search | Noindex/nofollow/remove those receivers; redirect where they have value | Dilution across low-value receivers | Standard technical-audit finding | STUDY |
| L7 | Orphans (crawl ∖ sitemap diff) | Link them from relevant hubs or retire them deliberately | PageRank decays ~85% per hop; zero-click pages get deprioritized | Deep pages need ≥1 click/week to hold their index slot | FIELD |
| L8 | Bulk 301s pointing at the homepage | Redirect each URL to the closest-intent live page; parent category as fallback | 301 passes ranking only on close intent match | Prevents the sitewide dip that follows mass removal | STUDY |
| L9 | Out-of-stock or seasonal products carrying `noindex`, 301 or canonical while unavailable | Keep the URL at 200 with real content and no negative directive; on restock push it through an Atom/RSS feed plus dynamic internal links from high-crawl nodes | The crawl scheduler keeps the historical negative priority after the directive is removed; the current HTTP status does not reset it (mechanism: technical-checks.md A2) | Sitemap resubmission and 10 manual GSC submissions/day did not break the flag; a bare out-of-stock render with no directive triggers soft 404 and the same cycle. Skip duration: benchmarks.md; recovery horizon: measurement.md §J5 | FIELD |
| L10 | GSC "Discovered – currently not indexed" at scale | 72-hour push: contextual **body** links from the most-crawled pages (GSC Top Pages export), priority sitemap with fresh `lastmod`, a substantive 200+ word addition, one external dofollow from an indexed page, TTFB <200ms with no 5XX or redirect chains | Google withholds budget from URLs carrying no importance signal; body and hub links transmit it, footer, author-bio and comment links barely do | 70–80% indexed within 72h; the body-link lever alone lands in 24–48h; one product page sat 10 days in limbo, indexed on day 4 of the push, top-4 by day 30. Never run this on "Crawled – currently not indexed" — that is a rejection, not a discovery problem | FIELD |
| L11 | Image URLs change per deploy, or the format is swapped at an unchanged URL | Serve a stable backend-proxied URL (`/product/{id}/image`) carrying the current bytes; 301 when a URL genuinely has to move | Image rankings attach to the image object; a new URL is a new object with no history | A product fell from positions 1–3 past 50 after an image URL change; serving `.webp` bytes at a `.png` URL degrades crawler handling over time | FIELD |
| L12 | Staging, dev or preview hosts in the index (subdomain enumeration, `site:` patterns) | HTTP auth or an IP allowlist on every non-production host, then remove the indexed URLs and re-check every subdomain | Blocked ≠ unindexed: a disallowed URL with any inbound link is indexed on anchor text alone and never sees the `noindex` | Duplicate copies of production competing on its own terms; the same enumeration is how competitors find unreleased work | CONFIRMED |
| L13 | Tracking-parameter URLs (`utm_*`, `gclid`, `fbclid`) named as the crawl-budget problem — or already sitting under a `Disallow` | Size the platform's own duplicate paths first (Shopify collection paths and `?variant=`); confirm the tracking share in the GSC Pages report by reason and in logs or Crawl Stats; lift the blanket `Disallow`; then strip tracking parameters from internal links and from affiliate and partner placements you control, and leave genuine external ones crawlable | Canonicals consolidate tracking variants by design, and *crawled, not indexed* is that tag working. A block cannot improve consolidation — a blocked URL never sees the canonical — and cannot touch the one real failure case, a parameterized URL out-signalling its canonical (mechanism: technical-checks.md A2, "Tracking parameters are not facets") | The wrong fix is the common one: a store where tracking URLs were crawled but never indexed had the canonicals already working, while the platform's collection and variant paths were the untouched hole. Blocking pays nothing back and costs the referral attribution and equity of real third-party links | CONFIRMED (mechanism); the single-store reading is FIELD |

## Gains — earn more visibility

| # | Trigger | Play | Mechanism | Observed | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | Page type mismatches the SERP | Rebuild to the dominant format; interlink commercial → transactional | Intent match drives satisfaction signals | Positions 12–20 → 3–7, bounce 78% → 34%, CVR 0.4% → 3.2% | FIELD |
| G2 | Thin information gain (few unique numbers) | Add original measurements/analytics; answer the questions the top-3 skip | Consensus is free; only single-source data adds value | 15+ unique data points ≈ 62/100 vs 40/100 | STUDY |
| G3 | No task completion on-site | Add the calculator/comparison/tool that finishes the job | Task completion correlates ρ=0.381 with growth; four features → 68% win rate | Multi-site core-update analysis | STUDY |
| G4 | Answer buried, multi-claim sentences, JS-gated facts | Front-load the answer, one claim per sentence, facts in plain HTML, 4–10 subheads, factual `alt` text | Lead bias, entity/triple extraction, raw-HTML parsing | Title–query match and JSON-LD correlations (figures and samples in benchmarks.md). The JSON-LD number is a correlation, **not** a license to add markup — myths.md holds the schema stance | STUDY |
| G5 | Brand described differently across profiles | Normalize 8–12 third-party profiles to the homepage reference; re-check quarterly | Conflicting category signals blur the brand centroid | Prerequisite for consistent AI answers | STUDY |
| G6 | Cited but not recommended (ghost citations) | Brand as grammatical subject of insights; entity graph (`sameAs`, Wikidata, author markup); third-party mentions in recommendation context | Models select names from memory, then retrieve support | Named brands cited 53.1% vs 10.6% unnamed | STUDY |
| G7 | Stale relative to the SERP age percentile | Substantial update: new sections, new data, restructure to intent — never a date bump | Freshness is scored against the result pool | Title/section/link changes count; `lastmod` does not | CONFIRMED |
| G8 | Slow or heavy money pages | LCP triage order (TTFB → clear the path → `fetchpriority` → defer JS), native `<dialog>` for modals | CWV plus satisfaction signals | 4.2s → 1.3s: bounce 67%→41%, CVR +93%, +8 positions in 6 weeks | FIELD |
| G9 | Weak reviews/sentiment in the sources models cite | Fix the product complaints models repeat; refresh the stale cited pages; report coordinated attacks | Reviews feed the model's verdict once "review" enters the fan-out | Mediocre ratings are the worst position — they give the model nothing to say | STUDY |
| G10 | Ranking in Google only, invisible in Bing | Fix Bing-side indexation and rankings deliberately | ChatGPT grounds heavily on Bing | A site past page 7 in Google earned 90k+ ChatGPT citations via Bing | FIELD |
| G11 | Long-form asset with no distribution | Atomize into ~60 platform-native pieces, each linking back, released over weeks | Distribution doubles as equity and independent entry points | Reported ~14× reach at zero content cost | FIELD |
| G12 | Local/service business with generic targeting | Neighborhood, landmark, urgency and "near me + qualifier" keyword layers built from Maps, PAA, autocomplete, GBP insights, support tickets | Specific intent beats broad location terms | Aggregate 1,840/mo at low competition, positions 2–5, 3× conversion vs the head term | FIELD |
| G13 | Programmatic pages planned | Three layers: template, data, **differentiation** (city-specific context generated from data fields, rotated structures); pre-publish gate (no empty variables, ≥800 words, valid schema, internal links, no near-duplicates); stagger publishing | Thin duplication triggers filters; specificity plus locality survives | 387 of 500 pages in the top-10 in 6 months in the cited case — with a closing window and rising risk | FIELD |
| G14 | PDFs ignored (manuals, datasheets, specs) | Bulk-compress and rewrite titles/metadata from extracted text | PDFs rank and are extractable | Measurable click lift per file | FIELD |
| G15 | Agentic surfaces unaddressed | Expose WebMCP tools (`/mcp/` page listing them, footer link; `llms.txt` may point at that page — as agent plumbing only, never sold as a ranking or citation lever, see myths.md) | Agents call structured tools instead of guessing from screenshots | Early-mover positioning; no ranking or citation claim | HYPOTHESIS |
| G16 | Traffic concentrated in one channel; publisher or lead-gen model exposed to zero-click | Build an **owned** surface (email list, community, Discord/Slack, Substack) and move engaged people onto it from rented platforms | Owned audiences are immune to SERP composition and platform algorithm changes; rented ones can vanish overnight | The one asset the 2026 practitioner panel agrees compounds; publishers with direct audiences kept revenue while click volume fell | STUDY |
| G17 | Buyers research the category on UGC platforms where the brand is absent | Presence on the platforms this audience actually uses (Reddit, YouTube, TikTok, Quora, Substack): study the conversations first, then contribute where engagement already exists | Those spaces are read by buyers **and** by models forming the category verdict; a Reddit comment was quoted by ChatGPT within 1h45m in one case | Reddit ≈40% of LLM citations (Semrush, 2026) — **treat the share as volatile, never as a plan input**: ChatGPT's Reddit share fell ~60% → 10% in six weeks after one retrieval change, and bulk auto-translated Reddit content was demoted after the May/June 2026 updates. Re-measure per engine before sizing anything (benchmarks.md, algorithm-updates.md). Platform weight also differs by engine — Reddit heavy in ChatGPT, light in Perplexity. Review/UGC sources drive the sentiment verdict | STUDY |
| G18 | One content format only | Diversify formats for the same niche — newsletter, video, podcast, first-hand testing — rather than more articles | Depth for a niche audience beats breadth; video and recorded opinion are the formats AI cannot replicate | Panel consensus 2026; survivors of the four-year blog collapse clustered on first-hand formats | STUDY |
| G19 | Phone-heavy or offline-closing business with untracked calls | Call tracking with dynamic number insertion per source, missed-call coverage, offline conversion import back into the ad platforms | Highest-intent conversions never reach the reporting model; bidding and content decisions optimize the wrong thing | One channel's conversions found to be 80–90% phone; missed calls cut 30%→21% lifted consultations; ~10% more closed leads just from tracking them | FIELD |
| G20 | Commercial templates with no decision accelerators | Ship the elements that close: crawlable pricing table, comparison naming real alternatives, implementation timeline, integration guide, ROI calculator, industry case study with numbers | ~70% of the buying process happens before contact; these assets are the sales conversation, and answer engines extract them | B2B AI-Overview encounter and cited-source click rates (undated 2025 vendor figures, B2B buyers only — quote them from benchmarks.md with that caveat, and never against the ~1% in-answer click rate, which measures all sessions); JS-gated pricing hands your numbers to aggregators | STUDY |
| G21 | Paid and organic run in separate rooms | Align ad and organic messaging per query cluster, feed paid search-term data into content planning, share landing pages and first-party data, forecast organic space against AI-era ad formats | Buyers do not distinguish channels; AI answers and expanded ad formats change how much organic space a query has left | A large share of AI Overviews link to Google-owned properties (undated 2025 vendor figure — benchmarks.md); mismatch interrupts the journey rather than accelerating it | STUDY |
| G22 | The first ~100 words of the page **source** are navigation, hero copy or marketing adjectives instead of the answer | Open the body with a ≤75-word factual summary — full entity name in the first sentence, no "innovative / world-class" filler — visible to users and first in the DOM | Lead bias: the opening of the document carries far more parsing weight, and the first read is spent top-down inside a ~5,700-char budget (architecture-and-equity.md) | Practitioner protocol reports roughly 10× weight on the opening block. Never relocate the block with CSS so humans see something else — hidden text is demoted and the split view is cloaking | FIELD |
| G23 | Invisible in Claude while visible elsewhere; templates that fail Brave's `validDoubleFetch` | Title present in every fetch, self-referencing canonical identical for anonymous and authenticated requests, anonymous/authenticated HTML delta inside 10–90%, structural content rendered before 5s | Claude's links overlap Brave's organic top ~86.7%; Brave's Web Discovery Project scores behavior and discards a page outright when the double-fetch check fails | WDP ignores backlinks, social signals, domain age and schema, and throttles every event to one increment per second, so click farming cannot move it | STUDY |
| G24 | Brand-new entity absent from every category answer, with no third-party sources for a model to retrieve | Earn press and analyst coverage, publish one factual category-defining page, target the narrow query rather than the head term | Published promotion bridges *unknown → mentioned* only where the model has a category gap; accumulated trust decides whether a citation becomes a recommendation | 34 pages / 5 domains / 9,886 answers (Feb–May 2026): a new conference filled 72 previously empty answer slots, 82% citing the new pages, while an established tool got 6%; narrow intent 66.4% vs 15.8% broad. The self-ranking listicle is the opposite play — see myths.md | STUDY |
| G25 | Mention volume spiked around a controversy while the brand vanished from "best X" and other commercial prompts | Fix the substance first, then publish neutral first-party comparison data and earn coverage that places the brand among ordinary category peers; re-measure recommendation share per funnel stage | Models attach the entity to the semantic cluster of the discussion; raw mention volume does not overwrite a negative category label | The documented mention-spike-versus-commercial-collapse case: benchmarks.md for the figures, entity-and-brand.md G4 for the cluster mechanism, measurement.md §J3b for the two metrics it forces apart | FIELD |
| G26 | RU-market site whose AEO work covers Western engines only | Run the prompt set against Alice AI (Алиса) / Yandex as well; for commerce, evaluate Yandex Commerce Protocol as a distribution decision, not an SEO one | Model popularity ≠ channel value — the engine that answers the query is not always the one that sends the click | RU study (72.4M AI referrals, 3,000 users surveyed, 2026-06): ChatGPT leads awareness and usage while Alice AI drives ~5× more clicks to sites; 42.8% start complex research inside an AI | STUDY |
| G27 | Discover or news is a material traffic source | Ask readers to mark the site as a preferred source, claim the publisher/creator Search profile, and build for being the **lead item** of a merged AI summary card rather than one of its sources | Loyalty surfaces survive SERP-composition changes; merged cards collapse several publishers into one slot | Google reports 2× more visits after a user marks a source, and 200k+ sites marked; Discover feed +58% YoY with social ~25% of it, and publishers reporting falling CTR from June 2026 | STUDY |

## Editorial and process plays

| # | Trigger | Play | Observed | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Content ships slowly and inconsistently | Role split (strategist / writer / SME 48h comment-only review / editor / SEO / publisher), standard brief, async review, automated pre-publish QA gate (keyword in H1 and first paragraph, meta ≤160 chars, ≥3 internal links, images ≤100KB, readability floor, CTA present) | 4 → 40 posts/month at constant headcount, 8-day cycle | FIELD |
| P2 | AI-assisted writing | Human-edited AI (not pure generation); source-grounded prompting; inline citation required for every external claim; automated fact-check pass; hallucinated-URL sweep | AI-assisted beat pure generation ~5× across Google, ChatGPT, AIO, Gemini; statistics hallucinate ~40% of the time unsupervised | STUDY |
| P3 | New site or new topic | Narrow niche → 50–100 subtopics → hub + a 10–15 page cluster (keeping the hub's own body to 5–10 out-links; sizing rule in architecture-and-equity.md fix 9) → 3–5 internal links from indexed pages to every new article → front-loaded publishing cadence | 47 top rankings and 0 → 12k organic/month in 6 months | FIELD |
| P4 | Link acquisition needed | Mine competitors' weakest donors, community placements, guest-author trails, podcasts (500–5k listeners), newsjacking, academic resource pages; relevance over DR; natural, event-driven velocity | 38% outreach success, 29 links at avg DR 35 in one campaign | FIELD |
| P5 | An update rolled out, or a decline needs attribution | Run the update-response protocol from algorithm-updates.md: exact dates → before/after export by page, query, country, device → segment by template and intent → competitor set for the same window → classify winner/loser/unchanged → only then hypothesize | Rollout ≠ effect window; spam and core recovery paths differ; GSC outages cluster on rollouts | Prevents the most expensive audit error — a confident plan built on a misattributed cause | CONFIRMED |
| P6 | AI-assisted refresh program; page diffs show previously ranking passages rewritten away | Bound the edit (title/H1 to intent, intro for snippet capture, entity and FAQ blocks, internal links) instead of commissioning full rewrites; role-isolated critique → revision → synthesis with blind judging; ground the loop in GSC queries, sales-call and support language, and GA4 conversion data; hold 14 days of GSC before locking a winner | The failure mode is the model quietly deleting the specific sentences that earned the ranking; one template-level fix propagates to thousands of programmatic pages | FIELD |
| P7 | Embeddable widget distributed to earn backlinks | `nofollow` the widget link, `noindex` the iframe page you control, and keep the widget for the referral traffic it actually earns | Widget links are named in Google's link-spam guidance and discounted; a sidebar widget across thousands of domains drew a penalty and its traffic never fully returned — the blast radius scales with the footprint | CONFIRMED |
| P8 | Backlink export shows flat linear velocity, a sudden burst, or an over-optimized anchor profile | Move acquisition to event-driven earned links (launches, research, PR) and hold the anchor mix and velocity inside the bands in **threats-and-defense.md I6**, which owns those numbers and the niche-relative reading of "toxic"; treat any purchased velocity as the risk it is | Documented penalty-versus-safe velocity pair and the distribution bands: threats-and-defense.md I6 | FIELD |

## Rules that override the table

- A play whose trigger is absent is noise — leave it out of the report.
- Never ship a FIELD or HYPOTHESIS play sitewide; run it as an experiment
  (experiments.md).
- Every play in the plan needs a verification step and an honest horizon
  (measurement.md, §J5).
- If a play conflicts with the myth list (myths.md), the myth list wins.
